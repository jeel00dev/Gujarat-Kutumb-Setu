# Gujarat Family ID: research-backed requirements discovery draft

**Version:** 0.6 · 20 September 2026  
**Status:** Proposed requirements for departmental workshops; **not** a government-approved SRS, legal interpretation, data-sharing authorization, database design, or implementation specification.  
**Scope of this document:** Functional and nonfunctional requirements, source evidence, and decisions needed to finalize them. `M` = proposed essential requirement; `C` = conditional on a government policy or signed integration; `F` = future capability to preserve in the requirements. Each requirement is proposed for Gujarat unless explicitly described as another state's practice.

## 2. Product boundary and actors

**Proposed purpose.** Maintain a trustworthy, current, statewide **family and person reference registry** and provide controlled integration to government programs. A Family ID links a family to people over time; a separate persistent person/member identifier prevents a split or transfer from creating a different person. The registry stores scheme references, status and evidence as authorized; **each scheme-owning department retains its legal eligibility decision, benefit calculation, payment and appeal authority** unless Gujarat explicitly delegates a particular function.

**Actors:** resident adult, nominated family representative, child/guardian, assisted-service operator, verifier, approver, corrections/grievance officer, department data steward, scheme administrator, integration administrator, privacy/security auditor, aggregate-data analyst and authorized government application. Define least-privilege scopes for each.

**Boundary cases to specify before approval:** single-person family; no ration card; no Aadhaar or failed Aadhaar verification; newborn, minor, orphan/guardian, senior with no mobile, person with disability; household worker/migrant/student; shared phone; death; marriage/divorce/separation; family with multiple residences or temporarily absent members; duplicate/incorrect PDS record; inter-state movement; family split/merge; contested membership or headship; change of address or bank details; overpayment and wrong scheme linkage; mixed-category families, an inter-caste marriage, an adopted child, an applicant with a revoked/disputed certificate, and an applicant whose caste appears on one government's list but not the relevant scheme's list. Rajasthan's [FAQ](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in/content/raj/janaadhaar/en/faqs1.html) and [SOP](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/doitassets/janaadhaar/PDF/SOP/Final%20Draft%20of%20SOP%20_Enrollment_Add%20Mem_%20Transfer_Split%20(080926)_1788853391225.pdf) show why lifecycle exceptions are ordinary operations.

## 3. Functional requirements

The following are **candidate Gujarat requirements**. The `C` items are deliberately conditional; the government must sign off on their policy and upstream access before implementation.

### A. Citizen entry, assisted entry and enrollment

| ID | Priority | Proposed requirement and observable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | M | Public home page explains what Family ID is, who can enroll, accepted channels, fees, privacy notice, required evidence, status tracking, corrections, accessibility and help contacts in Gujarati and English. |
| FR-02 | M | Resident can check by approved identifiers whether an existing family record may already exist **without exposing another household's details**; ambiguous matches go to review. |
| FR-03 | M | Resident can create a secure session and resume an unfinished application with automatically saved, access-controlled drafts, a stated retention period and a clear distinction between **saved** and **submitted**; only submitted applications receive a tracking reference distinct from the eventual Family ID. Shared-phone and no-mobile flows are available through supervised assisted channels. |
| FR-04 | M | The same policy and validation apply to self-service and government-approved assisted enrollment; the system records operator, channel, location, applicant and authorization. |
| FR-05 | M | Registration collects only the approved core fields for each person, family relationship, address, contact, representation, residency and supporting evidence; show where each item came from and who asserted it. |
| FR-06 | M | Support self-declared facts separately from documentary or source-verified facts, with verification state and effective dates; a prefilled field remains reviewable and correctable. |
| FR-07 | C | With signed access to Gujarat PDS, search and reconcile ration-card households, offer citizen confirmation of prefilled members and maintain the ration-card number as an external alias. Whether the public Family ID equals this number remains undecided. |
| FR-08 | M | Permit eligible non-PDS households to enroll by an independent route; a missing, suspended or disputed ration card cannot by itself block registration. |
| FR-09 | M | Where Gujarat requires supporting documents, offer secure upload or assisted capture with typed evidence, permitted alternatives, review outcome and expiry where relevant; where trusted source verification or authorized physical verification suffices, show that **no upload is needed**. Reject executable or unsafe uploads. |
| FR-10 | M | Validate required fields, ages, relationship and residence rules at the appropriate step, show clear Gujarati/English errors, permit correction before final submission, and issue a receipt. |
| FR-11 | M | Accept a single-person family and a family whose members do not all have personal phones; support a guardian/authorized representative for minors and incapable adults under approved rules. |
| FR-12 | C | Aadhaar-based authentication/e-KYC, offline verification or other national identity routes operate only where Gujarat has lawful purpose, notification/approval, technical permission and the necessary alternative path. Persist only legally permitted evidence and tokens; do not assume access to UIDAI's underlying database. |
| FR-13 | M | State clearly that Family ID itself does not confer scheme eligibility, ration entitlement, domicile, caste status or citizenship unless a separately authorized law or order says otherwise. |

### B. Family and member identity lifecycle

| ID | Priority | Proposed requirement and observable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| FR-14 | M | Issue an immutable registry Family ID only after the designated approval point; allow a provisional application/family reference beforehand if policy requires. Never silently recycle an identifier. |
| FR-15 | M | Issue a stable internal member/person identifier; maintain person-to-family membership with start/end dates and reason, so transfers and splits preserve identity and history. |
| FR-16 | M | Represent family head/representative as a time-bound role rather than an immutable property; changing the representative does not renumber the family. |
| FR-17 | M | Provide request flows for birth/add member, death, marriage, divorce, separation, departure, address change, relationship correction and representative change, with evidence and review as specified by Gujarat. |
| FR-18 | M | Support transfer of a person between families with controlled authorization, non-overlapping effective membership, source/target references, historical continuity and dispute handling. |
| FR-19 | M | Support family split, merge and deduplication without erasing old records; publish successor/predecessor links and a deterministic current-record lookup to authorized departments. |

### H. Caste/community certificates and category-based services

**Key distinction:** A family's registry entry is a *link* to people and evidence. Caste/community status and certification concern a **particular person**, a competent authority, an applicable list/jurisdiction and a point in time. Income/non-creamy-layer conditions are separate determinations. A Gujarat [SC scholarship example](https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/dscw/schemes/1521) has both student-category and income criteria; the [state SC list](https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/Dscw/About-Scheduled-Castes?lang=English) demonstrates synonyms and location-specific entries. Rajasthan's [SOP](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/doitassets/janaadhaar/PDF/SOP/Final%20Draft%20of%20SOP%20_Enrollment_Add%20Mem_%20Transfer_Split%20(080926)_1788853391225.pdf) explicitly distinguishes caste self-declaration at registration from certificates for a caste-dependent benefit. Haryana's [Family ID authority](https://hppa.haryana.gov.in/Caste-Class-certificate.html) describes a streamlined certificate service once category is verified in its own registry; that is **Haryana's arrangement**, not automatic Gujarat law.

### L. Scheme subjects beyond the registry family

The [Rajasthan scheme directory](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in/content/raj/janaadhaar/en/performance-dashboard/jan-aadhaar-services1.html) lists an animal-husbandry scheme; [Karnataka's service portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/) exposes electricity-scheme application and de-linking. The **proposed Gujarat capability** is to join an authorized scheme's own subject to a person/family without turning the family registry into a power-utility, land or livestock master database.

| ID | Priority | Proposed requirement |
|---|---|---|
| FR-138 | C | For an approved utility, dwelling or premises benefit, reference the **issuing authority's connection/consumer/premises identifier** and its verified occupant, owner, tenant or authorized beneficiary over a dated period; account for multiple connections, shared meters, moves, tenancy changes, duplicate claims, disconnection and de-linking. The utility/scheme owner alone validates the service point and decides the subsidy; a family address or Aadhaar link alone does not prove a person's entitlement to that connection. |
| FR-139 | C | For an approved asset-linked benefit (for example a registered land parcel, livestock/herd/animal, licensed enterprise or crop), link the owner authority's **typed external reference** to the named applicant and scheme-defined right (ownership, possession, cultivation or keeper role), validity dates, source evidence and dispute status. Do not create or infer title, animal identity, land extent or eligibility from household membership; a source correction and grievance must propagate without renumbering the family. |
| FR-140 | M | When a scheme defines a unit smaller than a registry household, derive and record the **assessed member set and eligibility-contributor set as of the assessment date**, rule/relationship versions, dependency criteria and unresolved relationship disputes. Permit two qualifying beneficiaries within one extended household when the scheme permits it; return pending where mandatory relationships cannot be established. The owning scheme approves any income or exclusion inference from this subset. |

## 4. Data collection requirements for the future forms

These are **field categories and collection rules**, not tables or a final form layout. Gujarat should approve a field dictionary, source and legal purpose *before* making a field mandatory.

| Category | Minimum candidate fields / rule | Sensitive or conditional fields |
|---|---|---|
| Family | Family reference, creation/status, start/end dates, geographic location code, current representative, declared residence | Dwelling coordinates or property identifiers only if specifically justified. |
| Person | Stable internal member ID, name (Gujarati and standardized display where appropriate), date of birth or supported age estimate, relationship, membership dates, life status | Aadhaar number or token, photo, gender, disability, caste, religion, marital status: collect only as authorized and needed. |
| Contacts and access | Reachable channels, phone ownership/shared-phone flag, preferred language, representative/guardian authority | Email/phone must not be presumed unique to a person or family. |
| Evidence | Document type, issuer, reference, date, verification status, hash/reference to protected content | Bank account, income record, biometric/e-KYC result, caste/domicile certificate under department-specific policy only. |
| Linked records | Source system, external identifier type/value (protected), source timestamp, confidence, conflict status | PDS status is a source fact; it is **not** an assertion that every household is eligible for every program. |
| Benefits and decisions | Scheme/department, rule and decision versions, dates, status, benefit reference, grievance link | Monetary amounts and bank details only if needed for the authorized use case; payment owner remains the relevant department. |
| Proactive leads | Person/family target, scheme and stage, trigger and assessment date, eligible/unknown reason, fact reference/freshness, action taken | Age, caste, disability, medical or income signals are disclosed only when the approved scheme needs them; **candidate** is not a final legal award. |

### Candidate intake and evidence matrix for applicants without ration cards

**These are proposed field groups, not a final mandatory-documents list.** UP's [district notice](https://ghaziabad.nic.in/%E0%A4%AB%E0%A5%88%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%880%E0%A4%A1%E0%A5%800-%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%95-%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0-%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%95-%E0%A4%AA/) describes an Aadhaar/mobile route for **UP**; Rajasthan's [FAQ](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in/content/raj/janaadhaar/en/faqs1.html) and [2026 SOP](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/doitassets/janaadhaar/PDF/SOP/Final%20Draft%20of%20SOP%20_Enrollment_Add%20Mem_%20Transfer_Split%20(080926)_1788853391225.pdf) enumerate different state-specific identity, residence, child and bank rules. Gujarat's owners must approve a per-scenario matrix before a developer creates required fields.

| Form stage | Candidate information | Candidate evidence and alternatives | Decision still needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check existing record | Any approved family, member, mobile, PDS or other authorized reference | Server-side confidential match; assisted review if unclear | Allowed search identifiers, confidence threshold and duplicate appeal. |
| Applicant/representative | Name, DOB/age evidence, relationship to others, preferred language, address/contact, representation basis | Approved identity method and, if acting for others, relevant guardianship or authorization | Which adult may apply and whether any identity method is mandatory. |
| Each member | Person name, DOB/age, relationship and residency; optional separate contacts | Birth record for child, accepted identity/age records for adult, alternate evidence if unavailable | Accepted proofs and exceptions by age and vulnerability. |
| Residence | Structured address and jurisdiction, current/temporary status | Approved residence proof or field verification; non-property-owning residents can use alternatives | Residency definition, proof list and who can certify local residence. |
| Optional scheme profile | Occupation, income, education, caste/community and certificate details only for an authorized purpose | Income/caste/domicile certificates as issued by their owners when needed | Which fields are collected at baseline versus upon using a named service. |
| Payments | Bank beneficiary and authorized account reference only for an actual financial use case | Approved account validation managed by payment owner | Whether any account belongs in Family ID at all; avoid a default bank requirement. |
| Submit/track | Declarations, notices, evidence checklist, receipts, application reference | Secure status challenge, supported assisted inquiry | Acknowledgment language, fees, verification levels, service time limit and escalation. |

**Explicit validations:** Never treat mobile number, address, family-head Aadhaar or ration number as universally unique identifiers. Preserve source-specific spelling/transliteration and aliases; separate *unknown* from *false*; model time and jurisdiction; allow one member's contested identity without corrupting every other family fact. In particular, an asserted community, a legally issued personal certificate, a category on a specific government list, non-creamy-layer status and an income-based eligibility result are distinct facts with different authorities and update cycles.

## 7. Recommended discovery evidence and acceptance gates

1. Obtain orders/notifications and data-sharing agreements, the Gujarat PDS field dictionary and sample quality report, civil registry specifications, existing e-District/Jan Seva or Digital Gujarat integration docs, certificate authority interfaces and category lists, **per-scheme legal rules and reporting contracts**, host security baseline and a list of data owners. Obtain one representative pension, scholarship, PDS, health, employment, marriage, DBT and **utility/asset-linked** implementation record, including actual forms, approval states and source schema, for signed traceability.
2. Interview Food and Civil Supplies, Planning, IT/e-Governance, Revenue/certificate issuers, Tribal Development, SC/SEBC welfare owners, local bodies, Women and Child, Health, Education, Finance/DBT, civil registration, district verifiers, accessibility representatives and citizens who lack phones/Aadhaar/ration cards.
3. Have policy owners approve D-01 through D-46 and a definition/evidence matrix for each field; test 40+ real-world scenarios (including split, move, death, ration-card replacement, later ration acquisition, mixed-category family, unverified caste, certificate revocation, missing Aadhaar/bank, missed matches, fraud, outage, incorrect passbook entry, income-year change, **age-threshold lead followed by consent/sanction**, separate qualifying nuclear units in one joint household, pension offset/top-up, moved electricity connection or tenant, contested land/animal linkage and appeal) against the requirements before database and form design. Require each field's subject, authority, optionality, effective dates, sensitivity, permissible consumers and correction owner to be signed off; this reduces later migrations but cannot guarantee that future legal or scheme changes will never require one.
4. Run a pilot with reconciled PDS plus non-PDS enrollment, assisted and self-service channels, one department read integration, one **scheme candidate-to-referral-to-decision-to-benefit feedback** flow and one correction flow; measure duplicate rate, coverage gaps, false rejection, candidate accuracy, turnaround, report completeness and source sync quality before statewide rollout.
5. Convert each `M` into a signed acceptance test and owner, each `C` into a tracked dependency/approval, and each `F` into a roadmap decision. Re-baseline the SRS when law, eligibility rules or authoritative source agreements change.

### SRS baseline and traceability gate before schema design

This is a **discovery draft**, not a signed software contract. Before modeling tables or forms, create one approved baseline record per requirement: stable ID; policy owner and implementing owner; normative requirement with one demonstrable outcome; rationale and directly inspected source (or `proposed`); dependency/decision ID; priority; actor and access scope; information subject and authoritative source; normal/exception flow; observable acceptance test; operating threshold and test method if nonfunctional; privacy/legal sign-off; version/effective date; and state (`proposed`, `approved`, `deferred`, `replaced`). Record a change's former ID and reason instead of silently renumbering it. The scenario matrix should link every life event, PDS/no-PDS route, caste certificate and scheme transition to requirements, government decisions and acceptance tests. Treat performance targets in NFR-01–05 as **proposals** until government measurement, not promises.

**Explicitly exclude from the base enrollment schema unless authorized:** a default family caste/category, compulsory Aadhaar or bank data for every member, compulsory birth/marriage/death certificate uploads for every scenario, a universal 200 KB limit, a universal shared-kitchen rule, a hard-coded UP/Haryana identifier size, an automatic eligibility/payment field, universal clinical profile, utility consumer number, land/animal register or universal midnight sync. Source-owned references, separate person/family identities, provenance, typed external subject links and effective-dated relationships are the durable concepts to approve first.
